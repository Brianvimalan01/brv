## The project in plain words

A public-sector organisation was replacing its old financial system with a modern ERP. That meant agreeing what the new system had to do, moving years of data across, connecting it to many other systems, and getting everyone ready for the switch. My job was to keep all of those moving parts (business teams, vendors, data, integrations and testing) pointed at the same go-live.

## How we kept it on track

Most of the day-to-day was running the project's RAID logs, which track risks, actions, issues and decisions. A few things made them work:

- **Every risk got a score.** Probability and impact were each rated 1 to 5 and multiplied, so a 20 stood out from a 4 and the team knew where to look first.
- **Risks became issues when they landed.** A risk that materialised moved to the issue log with its root cause and a resolution plan, so nothing lost its history.
- **Decisions were traceable.** Each one was tied to the progress meeting where it was made, with who proposed it and who approved it. Scope calls, such as bringing a module back into the implementation, all had a clear trail.
- **Actions had owners and dates.** Items from every working committee meeting were tracked to closure, then rolled up into monthly status reports for leadership.
- **Logs in two languages.** Entries were kept in Malay and English so business users and technical teams could both work from them.

The risks we watched most closely were familiar ones: deliverables and document sign-offs running late, user acceptance testing slipping, working-level users not fully understanding the agreed requirements, and requirement changes adding cost and time. A requirements traceability matrix helped keep that last one in check.

## Engagement Overview

A large-scale public-sector financial system modernization programme involving ERP implementation, business-process transformation, data migration, enterprise integration, vendor coordination, testing, organizational readiness, and transition to a future-state platform.

## Workstream A: Project Management Office and Delivery Governance

### Responsibilities

- Directed project governance and delivery management across business, technical, vendor, testing, security, integration, migration, and change workstreams.
- Managed Agile delivery routines, integrated planning, RAID governance, milestone tracking, dependencies, decisions, and executive reporting.
- Established project controls and governance mechanisms that supported proactive escalation, issue resolution, and management decision-making.
- Coordinated cross-functional teams and maintained alignment between programme objectives, implementation activities, and go-live priorities.
- Developed an integrated master schedule spanning documentation, development, integration, migration, testing, readiness, and deployment activities.
- Supported weekly reporting to working committees and senior leadership.

### Key Deliverables

- Integrated project plan and master schedule
- RAID, dependency, decision, and action logs
- Milestone and workstream dashboards
- Executive and governance reporting packs
- Working committee progress updates
- Go-live readiness and Go/No-Go governance materials

### Outcomes and Scale

- Supported end-to-end governance through implementation and go-live readiness.
- Coordinated delivery across 18 submodules and a complex multi-system integration landscape.
- Maintained structured oversight of interdependent functional and technical workstreams.

## Workstream B: Business Requirement Study and Business Analysis

### Responsibilities

- Conducted a Business Requirement Study across six ERP functional domains.
- Reviewed and restructured client-prepared documentation into clear, organized, vendor-ready specifications.
- Facilitated workshops with finance, operations, business SMEs, IT teams, and vendors to assess current-state processes and define future-state requirements.
- Translated business needs into functional, technical, integration, data, reporting, and change requirements.
- Defined bespoke requirements for organizational hierarchy, master-data governance, and a centralized community directory capability.
- Produced process flows, data definitions, business rules, and acceptance criteria to support solution build and testing.
- Maintained traceability from business need through solution design and implementation documentation.

### Key Deliverables

- Business Requirement Study
- Functional and technical requirement specifications
- Current-state and future-state process flows
- Gap analysis and business-process recommendations
- Data definitions, business rules, and acceptance criteria
- Requirements traceability and validation records

### Outcomes and Scale

- Covered six ERP modules or functional domains.
- Improved the structure and usability of the majority of the client's preliminary requirement documentation.
- Reduced ambiguity between business stakeholders, technical teams, and implementation vendors.

## Workstream C: ERP Data Migration, Integration, and Data Governance

### Responsibilities

- Led migration lifecycle planning across discovery, assessment, mapping, cleansing, design, build, testing, execution, validation, and reconciliation.
- Developed migration and integration plans covering more than 180 integration touchpoints across 18 submodules, internal systems, and external-party platforms.
- Conducted data discovery across legacy and modern systems to identify incomplete, duplicate, inconsistent, and invalid records.
- Defined source-to-target mappings, transformation rules, cleansing requirements, validation checks, and acceptance criteria.
- Reviewed master-data attributes, business rules, ownership, and governance processes with business data owners.
- Documented data issues, validation results, owners, remediation actions, and resolution rationale.
- Facilitated technical workshops to align integration requirements, architecture decisions, application ownership, and data governance.

### Key Deliverables

- Data migration strategy and lifecycle plan
- Integration blueprint and touchpoint inventory
- Source-to-target mapping specifications
- Data-cleansing and remediation requirements
- Data-quality rules and validation controls
- Reconciliation and issue-tracking records
- Data ownership and governance documentation

### Outcomes and Scale

- Covered 180+ integration touchpoints across 18 submodules.
- Included 6+ internal legacy and modern systems and 6+ external-party platforms.
- Strengthened traceability, quality controls, and readiness for migration execution.
