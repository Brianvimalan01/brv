## Role Coverage

- Project Management Lead / PMO Lead
- Business Analyst
- Data Migration and Integration Facilitator
- Technical Consultant
- Technology Transformation Roadmap Consultant
- Vendor and Solution Evaluation Support

### Responsibilities

- Directed project governance and delivery management across business, technical, vendor, testing, security, integration, migration, and change workstreams.
- Managed Agile delivery routines, integrated planning, RAID governance, milestone tracking, dependencies, decisions, and executive reporting.
- Established project controls and governance mechanisms that supported proactive escalation, issue resolution, and management decision-making.
- Coordinated cross-functional teams and maintained alignment between programme objectives, implementation activities, and go-live priorities.
- Developed an integrated master schedule spanning documentation, development, integration, migration, testing, readiness, and deployment activities.

### Key Deliverables

- RAID, dependency, decision, and action logs

- Supported end-to-end governance through implementation and go-live readiness.

- Maintained structured oversight of interdependent functional and technical workstreams.

### Key Deliverables

- Business Requirement Study

- Data definitions, business rules, and acceptance criteria

- Covered six ERP modules or functional domains.

- Reduced ambiguity between business stakeholders, technical teams, and implementation vendors.

### Outcomes and Scale

- Strengthened traceability, quality controls, and readiness for migration execution.

# Project 2: Cyber Incident Management PMO | Banking, Malaysia

## Engagement Overview

A five-month PMO and governance engagement supporting the management of a cybersecurity incident in a regulated banking environment.

## Role

PMO Lead / Cyber Incident Management PMO

## Responsibilities

- Coordinated governance and executive reporting across incident analysis, containment, remediation, recovery, and closure activities.
- Consolidated incident status, business impact, remediation progress, technology-risk exposure, decisions, and escalations for leadership visibility.
- Established structured tracking for actions, risks, dependencies, owners, decisions, and due items.
- Coordinated Information Security, Technology, Risk, Business, and specialist stakeholders.
- Supported root-cause-analysis governance and corrective-action tracking without positioning the role as deep forensic or SOC investigation work.
- Maintained traceability and accountability across remediation and control-improvement activities.
- Used AI-assisted analysis and dashboarding approaches to consolidate incident insights, risks, actions, and management reporting.

## Key Deliverables

- Cyber incident governance framework
- Executive incident-status dashboard
- Action, risk, dependency, decision, and escalation logs
- Remediation progress reporting
- Root-cause and corrective-action governance records
- Closure and control-improvement tracking

## Outcomes

- Improved leadership visibility across incident impact and remediation status.
- Strengthened owner accountability, escalation discipline, and cross-functional coordination.
- Supported incident recovery, closure, cyber-resilience, and control-improvement activities.
